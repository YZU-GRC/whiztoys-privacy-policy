WhizToys PLUS+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

Welcome to WhizToys PLUS+ (“the App”). The App is published and operated by the Gerontechnology Research Center (“GRC”) at Yuan Ze University (YZU), Taoyuan, Taiwan. By using the App, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy (the “Policy”). If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Policy applies when you download, install, register for, sign in to, and use the App, and covers how GRC collects, processes, uses, retains, and protects your personal data in connection with account services, any cloud features, and related value-added services GRC offers, as actually provided. If GRC links to this page from the App or from GRC’s website, the version published here (with its “last updated” date) controls.

This Policy does not apply to third-party sites, applications, or ads operated by others, or to content you open outside GRC’s control. Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies. If you are a minor, you should use the App with a parent or legal guardian’s consent and involvement.

2. Collection, processing, and use of personal data

GRC only processes personal data in a fair manner and for defined purposes, and where GRC has a valid legal basis under applicable law (e.g. consent, contract, legitimate interests, or legal obligation, as the case may be).

(1) Categories of data GRC may collect or process

  • Account and registration data — e.g. email, display name; passwords are handled by the identity service and are not stored by GRC in clear text. The App may use Amazon Web Services (AWS) Cognito or similar.
  • Game and activity records — scores, ability-related metrics (e.g. reaction, concentration, muscle endurance, memory, coordination), training plans, leaderboards, and analytics, including data from WhizToys interactive mat hardware when connected.
  • Profile and player settings — nickname, avatar, date of birth, gender, notes, and other fields you provide (as available in the product).
  • Device and connection data — device type, OS, App version, logs, IP address, and technical identifiers or status for Bluetooth Low Energy and MQTT to pair and sync with peripherals.
  • Camera, photo library, microphone — only when you use those features and grant OS permissions, as required for the feature, unless you separately consent to other uses.

(2) Purposes of processing and use

  • Provide, maintain, and improve games, training, cognitive activities, hardware connectivity, and account services; show history, charts, leaderboards, and (where applicable) cloud sync.
  • Authenticate users, protect against abuse and fraud, and maintain security; comply with law and competent authorities’ requests.

GRC does not use your personal data for marketing or profiling that is clearly unrelated to these purposes, except where you have agreed or the law allows.

(3) Cookies and similar technologies

When you use web-based login or related web views in the App, GRC or its processors may use cookies or local storage for session, security, or preferences. You can adjust browser or device settings, which may limit some features.

(4) Your rights

Depending on applicable law (e.g. Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act, GDPR, CCPA, where applicable), you may have rights to access, rectify, delete, restrict, object, port data, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. In-app account deletion or export, if offered, is described in the App. You may contact GRC using the controller and contact information at the bottom of this page; GRC may need to verify your identity.

3. Protection of data

GRC uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your personal data, such as transport encryption (e.g. HTTPS/TLS, as implemented), access controls, staff training, and reviews. Please also protect your credentials, device, and second factors.

Personal data may be stored on servers operated by GRC or by cloud providers and may be located outside Taiwan (e.g. AWS regions). Where GRC transfers data across borders, GRC uses safeguards permitted by law.

GRC retains data as long as needed for the purposes above, and longer if required for legal claims, security backups, or legal retention. When retention ends, or you delete your account and no law requires further retention, GRC deletes, stops processing, or anonymizes the data as appropriate.

4. External links

The App or web pages that GRC provides may contain links to third-party sites, partners, or ads. If you follow a link and leave the environment that GRC operates, the collection, processing, and protection of your data is governed by that third party’s policy, not this one. GRC encourages you to read their terms and privacy statements before you provide personal data.

5. Sharing with third parties

GRC does not sell your personal data. GRC may share it only as needed in these situations:

  • Processors and cloud infrastructure — e.g. AWS (including Cognito), backend services, databases, caches, and messaging, only to the extent needed to run the App, under contract and legal obligations.
  • Real-time / IoT — e.g. MQTT to sync with mats, devices, or services, subject to the feature implementation.
  • Future integrations — e.g. healthcare, care, or other third-party APIs: GRC will identify the partner, data categories, and purpose when the feature is enabled, and will obtain consent or another legal basis as required.
  • Legal and regulatory — GRC may disclose data where required by law, court, or a competent authority, or to protect GRC’s rights, users, or the public, within the limits of applicable law.

GRC contractually requires subprocessors to meet appropriate data protection standards. If you are concerned about a third party’s practices, also see Section 4 above.

6. Location-based services

The App’s core focus is games, fitness, and mat hardware. On some Android and iOS versions, the operating system may require or suggest location permission to support Bluetooth scanning and connecting nearby devices. GRC does not use this to build a long-term record of your movements, and GRC does not use location for map-based navigation or location-based ad fencing as a primary purpose. If the App does not offer a feature that shows your position on a map, GRC does not use precise location for ad tracking. If you deny or turn off the permission, you may be unable to discover or connect to the mat and some features may be unavailable.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

GRC may update this Policy for legal, technical, or product reasons. GRC will post the new “last updated” date on this page and, where practicable, notify you through the App, in-product messages, or email. Continued use after the update may mean you accept the new Policy, as allowed by law. If you do not agree, you may stop using the App and use account-deletion or contact GRC using the information at the bottom of this page. For questions about the Policy or your data, use the same contact information.
